Overview

125|

AEGIS is a privacy infrastructure protocol designed for Base, enabling on-chain activity to be shielded from public view using zero-knowledge proofs. It provides modular privacy tools for individuals, applications, and autonomous AI agents.

126|

The protocol consists of two core smart contracts: AegisShield for privacy pool management and AegisPool for liquidity provision.

127|
128| 129| 130|
131|

Why Privacy Matters

132|

On-Chain Surveillance

133|

Every transaction on public blockchains is permanently recorded and visible to everyone. This creates privacy risks for individuals and businesses who want confidential financial activity.

134| 135|

AI Agent Privacy

136|

Autonomous agents making financial decisions on-chain face surveillance risks. AEGIS shields agent transactions from public observation, enabling trustless autonomous operation.

137| 138|

Regulatory Compliance

139|

Privacy-preserving transfers help organizations comply with data protection regulations while maintaining blockchain transparency.

Core Concepts

145| 146|

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

147|

ZK proofs allow you to prove knowledge of information without revealing the information itself. AEGIS uses ZK proofs to verify transaction validity without exposing sender, recipient, or amount.

148| 149|

Privacy Pools

150|

AEGIS pools are shared liquidity pools where deposits are mixed. Withdrawals can come from the pool without revealing which deposit a withdrawal originated from, breaking the transaction chain.

151| 152|

Commitment Hash

153|

When depositing, you generate a commitment hash (Poseidon hash) derived from your secret and nullifier. This commitment is stored on-chain but reveals nothing about your deposit.

154| 155|

Nullifier

156|

To prevent double-spending, AEGIS tracks nullifiers. Each withdrawal uses a unique nullifier that is marked as spent, preventing the same note from being withdrawn twice.

157|

How It Works

162| 163|

Step 1: Deposit

164|

You send ETH to the AegisShield contract along with a commitment hash. Your funds are locked in the privacy pool. Your secret and nullifier remain unknown.

165| 166|

Step 2: Generate Proof

167|

When ready to withdraw, you generate a ZK proof that proves:

168|
    169|
  • You know the secret matching your commitment
    • 170|
  • Your nullifier hasn't been used before
    • 171|
  • You have sufficient balance
    • 172|
173| 174|

Step 3: Withdraw

175|

You submit the ZK proof to withdraw funds to any address. The smart contract verifies the proof without learning your identity or original deposit details. Your nullifier is marked as spent.

Security Model

201| 202|

Double-Spend Prevention

203|

Nullifier tracking prevents spending the same note twice. Once a note is withdrawn, its nullifier is marked as spent on-chain.

204| 205|

Proof Verification

206|

All ZK proofs are verified on-chain using cryptographic verifier contracts. Invalid proofs are rejected by the smart contract.

207| 208|

No Private Key Exposure

209|

Your secret and nullifier are never exposed to the blockchain. Only derived values (commitments, nullifiers) are recorded on-chain.

210| 211|

Amount Privacy

212|

Transaction amounts are hidden. Deposits and withdrawals appear identical on-chain, masking transaction size.

Frequently Asked Questions

276| 277|

Is AEGIS safe?

278|

AEGIS uses proven cryptographic techniques (ZK proofs, Poseidon hashing) to provide security guarantees. Smart contracts are audited and verified on-chain.

279| 280|

How much does it cost?

281|

Gas costs depend on network conditions. Base provides lower fees than Ethereum mainnet. Pool withdrawals incur a 0.5% protocol fee.

282| 283|

Is it really private?

284|

Yes. Your identity, transaction amount, and counterparty are hidden. Only ZK proofs verify transaction validity on-chain.

285| 286|

Can I withdraw to any address?

287|

Yes. AEGIS is designed to support withdrawals to any address. No KYC or restrictions.

288| 289|

What happens if I lose my secret?

290|

Your funds become unrecoverable. Store your secret securely. AEGIS is non-custodial — only you control access to your deposits.

291| 292|

Is this legal?

293|

AEGIS is a privacy technology. Legality depends on jurisdiction and use case. Consult local laws before using.
